What is Budgeta?

Budgeta is a simple personal finance tracker built around five pillars — Income, Savings, Spending, Investment, and Protection — so you can see at a glance where your money goes and track progress toward your goals. All your data stays on this device.

Data

Clear all transactions and goals and start again from zero. Your PIN and profile stay. Remove account deletes the PIN and everything else.

Tracker Guide

  1. Dashboard — see your 5-pillar totals and breakdown pie chart. Use the month dropdown to check one month, or All months for the grand total.
  2. Transactions — log income, savings, spending, investment, or protection entries, then filter your history by month.
  3. Goals — set savings targets and track progress toward each one.
  4. Growth — watch your overall balance trend over time.
  5. Settings — update your nickname, currency, appearance, PIN, and reminders.
